Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about ShramikPay products, services and digital business solutions.

1. What is ShramikPay?

ShramikPay provides digital software solutions including Labor Management Software, Loan Management Software, Android Apps and Custom Business Applications for Indian businesses.

2. Who can use Labor Management Software?

Contractors, construction companies, factories and businesses can use ShramikPay to manage employee attendance, wages, overtime, advances and payroll.

3. Does ShramikPay support loan and EMI management?

Yes. Finance companies and microfinance institutions can manage customers, KYC details, loan accounts, EMI collection, agents and financial reports.

4. Do you provide website and mobile app development services?

Yes. ShramikPay provides website development, Android app development, custom software, cloud solutions and business dashboards.

5. How can I request a demo?

You can contact ShramikPay through the website contact page or WhatsApp to schedule a free product demo.
